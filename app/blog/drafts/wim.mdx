## What is Maturity
#### October 29, 2025

In the writing of "Who Would Have a Blog", I came upon a conundrum. I wanted to boil down the goals of a value like the phrase "Everything is a work in progress" into the adjectives it invoked; words like passionate, eccentric, efficient. One word, mature, was a problem. To me, maturity and its synonyms, were poorly defined.

There is a vague categorization of the level of a term, that is especially hard to speak of because words are so thoroughly infested with connotations and one's past experience, and adjectives are especially suseptible. To try and define a word like "passionate" in terms of other adjectives is pretty difficult. I can say that "very interested" gets close but not enough, but some might say it gets plenty close enough. Personally, I think it be better described as "urgent interest". Disagreements like these are close to impossible to reocncile.
